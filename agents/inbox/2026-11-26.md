## RocksDB
- year: 2012
- kind: concept
- era: modern
- parent: lsm-tree
- creators: Dhruba Borthakur
- wikipedia: RocksDB
- summary: An embedded key-value store built on a log-structured merge-tree and tuned for multi-core processors and solid state drives. Dhruba Borthakur started it at Facebook in 2012 as a fork of Google's LevelDB, and it is used as a storage engine inside many other databases.

- confidence: high

## Red-black tree
- year: 1978
- kind: concept
- era: micro
- parent: b-tree
- creators: Leonidas J. Guibas, Robert Sedgewick
- wikipedia: Red-black tree
- summary: A self-balancing binary search tree in which one extra bit per node, its color, keeps search, insertion and deletion logarithmic. Rudolf Bayer invented its forerunner in 1972 as a special case of the B-tree, and Guibas and Sedgewick gave it its form and name in 1978.
- sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red%E2%80%93black_tree
- confidence: high

## Consistent hashing
- year: 1997
- kind: concept
- era: network
- parent: integrity
- creators: David Karger, Eric Lehman, Tom Leighton, Rina Panigrahy, Matthew Levine, Daniel Lewin
- wikipedia: Consistent hashing
- summary: A way of hashing in which adding or removing a server moves only a small share of the keys. David Karger and colleagues at MIT introduced the term in a 1997 paper on web caching, and two of the authors founded Akamai on it the next year.
- sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consistent_hashing
- confidence: high

- kind: network
- era: network
- parent: usenet
