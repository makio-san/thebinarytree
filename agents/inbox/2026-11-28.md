## Theano
- year: 2007
- kind: language
- era: modern
- parent: ai
- creators: none (MILA, University of Montreal)
- wikipedia: Theano (software)
- summary: A Python library and optimizing compiler for array math that ran on CPU or GPU, from the Montreal lab led by Yoshua Bengio. Deep learning frameworks that came later followed its approach, and major development ended with version 1.0 in November 2017.
