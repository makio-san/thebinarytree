## Verilog
- year: 1984
- kind: language
- era: micro
- parent: integrated-circuit
- creators: Prabhu Goel, Phil Moorby, Chi-Lai Huang
- wikipedia: Verilog
- summary: A hardware description language created at what became Gateway Design Automation between late 1983 and early 1984 to simulate digital circuits. It was opened up in the early 1990s, standardised as IEEE 1364 in 1995 and later merged into SystemVerilog.
- sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verilog
- confidence: high

## VHDL
- year: 1987
- kind: language
- era: micro
- parent: integrated-circuit
- creators: none (developed for the US Department of Defense VHSIC program)
- wikipedia: VHDL
- summary: A hardware description language begun in 1983 for the US Department of Defense to document the custom chips in its equipment, with syntax borrowed from Ada. It became the standard IEEE 1076 in 1987 and, with Verilog, is how most digital chips and FPGAs are designed.
- sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VHDL
- confidence: high
