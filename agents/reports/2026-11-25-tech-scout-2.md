## For a human

- chroot: confidence is medium because the article names no author, and the parent `research-unix-v6` is one version early. The call came in during Version 7 development.
- ext4: Theodore Ts'o is credited as the ext3 maintainer who announced the ext4 plan in 2006, not as a sole author. The year is the stable release, 2008.
- dBase sits under `relational` for want of a microcomputer database group. It is not strictly relational, so `pre-relational` is the alternative.
- InfiniBand sits under `beowulf` as a cluster interconnect. `pci-express` is the alternative home.
- Actor model and CSP both point at the `theory` group in foundations. Erlang and Go are their descendants and already in the tree.
- Perlin noise sits under the `graphics-documents` group in media. `shader` in games is the alternative.
- Signal Protocol under `diffie-hellman` reflects its key agreement. `pgp` is the alternative as earlier message encryption.
- ALOHAnet: `ethernet` and `wifi` descend from its random access idea, and the curator may want to move them under it.
