# Sources for the web reader

The feeds `web-reader` reads each week, grouped by what the site may take from them. One feed per line with a short note. Add a feed only when it has given good posts more than once, and put it under the heading its licence belongs to.

## Open licence or public domain

Text and pictures here may be reused with credit. A post can say a little more about these pieces and can show their photos (credit the author and the licence).

- https://theconversation.com/us/technology/articles.atom (The Conversation, CC BY-ND 4.0: quote it as written, never edit a quote)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=featuredfeed&feed=featured&feedformat=atom (Wikipedia featured article, CC BY-SA 4.0)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=featuredfeed&feed=onthisday&feedformat=atom (Wikipedia on this day, CC BY-SA 4.0)
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php?action=featuredfeed&feed=potd&feedformat=atom&language=en (Wikimedia Commons picture of the day, licence on each file page)
- https://www.nasa.gov/feed/ (NASA, mostly public domain; check each picture's page)
- https://rss.arxiv.org/rss/cs.CY (arXiv, computers and society; licence on each paper's page)

## Link only

All rights reserved by their authors. A post gives the title, the author, a short summary in its own words and the link. Never their text, never their pictures.
