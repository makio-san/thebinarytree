On 18 November the tree had 944 nodes. Today it has 1,018. Every one of the 74 new nodes was found by an agent, written into the dataset by another, checked by a third and published by a fourth. Nothing reached the site without a review.

## Old ideas that were missing

The scout does not only look for news. Several of the additions are decades old and should have been there from the start: the [Hamming code](node:hamming-code) from 1950, [Dijkstra's algorithm](node:dijkstra-algorithm), [Quicksort](node:quicksort), the [Cooley-Tukey FFT](node:cooley-tukey-fft) and the [Monte Carlo method](node:monte-carlo). The internet's plumbing filled in too, with [Telnet](node:telnet), [SMTP](node:smtp), [IMAP](node:imap), [NTP](node:ntp) and [Gopher](node:gopher).

## The frontier grew most

The frontier branch took the largest share: [LSTM](node:lstm), [ResNet](node:resnet), [TensorFlow](node:tensorflow) and [PyTorch](node:pytorch), then [ChatGPT](node:chatgpt), [Claude](node:claude), [Gemini](node:gemini), [Llama](node:llama) and [DeepSeek-R1](node:deepseek-r1). [AlphaFold 2](node:alphafold-2) and [Google Willow](node:google-willow) sit there as well, beside the [El Capitan](node:el-capitan) supercomputer.

## Security and the open web

Security now records some of its worst days, [Stuxnet](node:stuxnet), [Heartbleed](node:heartbleed) and [WannaCry](node:wannacry), next to the fixes that followed: [Let's Encrypt](node:lets-encrypt), [TLS 1.3](node:tls-1-3), [WireGuard](node:wireguard) and [passkeys](node:webauthn). The social web gained [Mastodon](node:mastodon), [ActivityPub](node:activitypub) and [Bluesky](node:bluesky).

## What the review caught

Not everything got in on the first try. On 21 November the reviewer rejected two branches over one date: the scout had Siri's app in December 2010, while SRI's own history says Apple bought Siri in April of that year. A missing node is better than a wrong one, so the branch waited.

## Four more agents

Until now a rejected branch simply sat there. From today a **branch fixer** picks it up, corrects what the review found and sends it back. A **lineage architect** plans whole new lineages the tree lacks, a **blog writer** will write posts like this one when enough has changed, and a **preview refresher** keeps the picture you see when the site is shared in step with the real counts. That makes twelve.
